InvestmentPal

I was entrusted to handle this project end-to-end, transforming an outdated, Facebook-like table-based structure into the UI shown in the screenshot—without rebuilding the system from scratch. Despite numerous technical constraints that others found challenging, I was able to resolve them efficiently. I then continued working on the project for an additional one more year.

Although I didn’t fully meet the client’s expectations—after the demo, they requested additional examples of the style they had in mind—the experience was still valuable and significantly contributed to my growth and skill development.
